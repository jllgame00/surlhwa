# Campaign authoring and encounter preservation

ACT 1 uses six short authored night blocks, preserving all 17 early comparison encounters: [01–03], [04–06], [07–09], [10–12], [13–16], [17]. This spreads teaching and service across shorter evenings; Prince retains first/follow-up/closing sequence. A new night is an authored quiet transition, not a difficulty or supply/knowledge reward. Times are player-driven; no arbitrary waiting gate. The final short closing block leaves space for farewell and memory. This reversible pacing proposal is for bundled Gate 2 review.

ACTs 2–7 below are source-preservation planning only. No runtime authoring or implementation before Gate 2. Their exact night placement remains later production work under the same approved ACT order. Each row remains required content provenance; none is silently dropped.
