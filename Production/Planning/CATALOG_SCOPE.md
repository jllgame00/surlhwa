External recipe facts were rechecked 2026-10-14 against the individual [IBA Negroni](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/negroni/) and [IBA Gin Fizz](https://iba-world.com/iba-cocktail/gin-fizz/) pages. Negroni is direct preparation over ice with gentle stirring. Gin Fizz shakes the first three liquids with ice and adds soda afterwards; its official garnish suggestion and unquantified splash are not silently treated as mandatory game rules. No universal conversion from splash/dash to ml is claimed.

The domain retains six-decimal actual ml and three 60/300/180 ml role capacities; the added shaker currently uses 300 ml. Negroni, Gin Fizz and Manwol totals fit the serving capacity. New supply identities remain separate: coffee liqueur, cream, vodka, Campari, lemon juice, simple syrup and soda water. Gin is not renamed London Dry Gin; legacy whiskey/vermouth and rye/sweet-red/dry-vermouth retain distinct IDs. No knowledge grant follows mere catalog or shelf inclusion.

Migration categories apply per source item: REUSE_AS_DATA for approved stable definitions; ADAPT_WITH_PROVENANCE for explicit adopted numeric/presentation differences; REIMPLEMENT_FROM_CANON for behavior; REFERENCE_ONLY for RC1 code/serialized implementation; DEFERRED for later campaign content; REJECTED for unsupported/obsolete behavior. These are dispositions, not permission to bulk migrate.

Hanky Panky, French 75 and Last Word: DEFERRED to owning ACTs after Gate 2; their source rows remain in CAMPAIGN_AUTHORING.md. Yeomyeong/Janseol: REFERENCE_ONLY historical prototypes. Full IBA catalog: DEFERRED; no bulk import, knowledge or supply. RC1 PlayerPrefs, MonoBehaviours and old scoring: REFERENCE_ONLY; no importer or architecture adoption. Timing/score gates on ordinary departure, all-perfect Prince farewell, memory trophies and global 3D-room presentation: REJECTED for this implementation.
