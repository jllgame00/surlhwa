# ACT 1 audio candidates and lifecycle

All cues remain provisional within the accepted quiet-bar direction. The licensed
ACT 1 art pass uses `ProductionArt/FootstepFeltWood.wav` on the existing contact
events and pitch/mix policy. The preceding `FootGrounded.wav` is preserved for
comparison. See `Manifests/audio-act1-drafts.json` for the preceding drafts and
`Manifests/act1-art-production-inventory.json` for current linked provenance.

| Context | Actual production behavior | Review boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bar/weather | Retained room and winter loops; lower room bed during reading, quieter during farewell. | Balance, fatigue and exterior impression need listening. |
| Actual liquid/technique | Physical loop only while a production step accepts liquid delivery or technique movement; modal/focus cancellation removes held input. | Current Pour/Stir/Strain references remain tool/audio debt. |
| Entrance/walk/seat | Entrance cue, felt/wood foot candidate on walk contact-frame changes, seat cue at seating; existing small pitch offsets retained. | Body, low-mid presence, transient, surface impression, cadence and mix remain OPEN. |
| Farewell | Lower ambience; no success fanfare or motif under the Prince's text. | Owner judges emotional pacing and quiet. |
| Earned memory | One sparse 22-second original procedural motif after actual completion. Both variants retain the same restrained musical vocabulary. | Notes, timbre, space and onset remain a composition draft. |
| Continue at completion | Existing memory is visible; motif is not replayed as a new grant. | No new progress/audio reward from loading. |
| Modal/focus/session removal | Physical loop stops; motif stops, with no background continuation. Ambience stays as the bar's quiet bed. | Final pause/mix treatment can be tuned within the accepted direction. |
| Master volume/mute | All five sources multiply the player's master setting; settings persist separately from campaign progress. | Objective mute/lifecycle checks do not approve the final mix. |

`FootGrounded.wav` and `FirstNightMotif.wav` use source-tracked procedural synthesis, with no imported recordings or third-party samples. Retained reference cues preserve their prior provenance. Internal authorship is not a blanket legal clearance for inherited art/audio.

The new `FootstepFeltWood.wav` is also original procedural synthesis, authored by
`Production/Tools/author_act1_footstep.py`; no new third-party recording is used.
The art-pass 720p actual Unity mix is stereo 48 kHz with zero clipped samples.
Its approximately 5 Hz visual recording cannot certify frame-accurate contact
synchronization. In-room listening is included in the bundled Gate 2 owner review;
PD07–PD09 stay OPEN. Separate source-cue comparison is in
[the source gallery](review-act1-art-source.html).
