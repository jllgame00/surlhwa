# Windows demo review

This package is for owner playtesting, not final public-release approval. Keep the whole Windows folder together. Start SURLHWA.exe and use the Korean New Game / Continue controls. README.md contains controls; CREDITS.md records provenance and remaining ownership checks.

## Human playtest

1. Play a fresh 5 / 4 / 4 / 4 run with physical mouse and keyboard, without editor/debug controls. Read clues before attempting each required recipe.
2. Read the Guide while stirring/shaking, then resume. Open Esc sound settings, adjust every slider and mute; confirm comfortable levels and pause/resume.
3. Fail a required discovery once and retry. In a second run, fail one Prince service; confirm quiet farewell. All three successful services should lead to open farewell. Wrong technique must not count as success even with a fitting cocktail.
4. In later recommendations try different learned/supplied cocktails and methods. Confirm the request feels open to interpretation, with no hidden single emotional answer.
5. Exit after a night, Continue, then finish night 4. Continue after completion must not create night 5. New Game resets story/knowledge but preserves sound settings.
6. Review Korean text, scrolling, dialogue choices, recipe quantities, card art and click targets at 1280×720 and 1920×1080.
7. Judge character scale/poses, winter room mood, music repetition/mix, dialogue cadence, emotional impact and four-night pacing.

## Current limits

- Input-seam automation proves production state transitions, not physical pointer hit-testing or subjective feel.
- Save resumes persisted night/knowledge/story state; no in-progress liquid or exact dialogue cursor save.
- Cocktail, character, backdrop and audio additions are drafts. Seated poses, refined glass/garnish visuals, additional dressing and a demonstration video remain presentation work.
- Prince statue eligibility is recorded; no statue is presented in the four-night demo.
- Other aspect ratios/platforms, later nights, reserve cocktail production and broader collection/economy systems are outside this release scope.
- Public distribution requires owner review of the pre-existing artwork's source/permission and final credits.
